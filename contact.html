Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our custom team wear and pro hardware services.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom team kits?

Our minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom sublimation jerseys is 10 units. We offer tiered bulk discounts for orders exceeding 50 and 200 units.

How long does the customization or gear tuning process take?

Standard sublimation printing and apparel orders are delivered in 5 to 7 business days. Racket stringing and bat knocking calibrations are completed in 48 to 72 hours.

Can I inspect a sample or digital mockup before printing?

Absolutely! We create high-resolution 3D digital mockups for your approval before scheduling the final printing and production.

Do you ship orders internationally?

Yes, we ship globally. Shipping costs and estimated delivery schedules depend on destination region and are included in your wholesale bulk quote.
