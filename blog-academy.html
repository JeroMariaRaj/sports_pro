How the Titans Academy Upgraded Their Entire Arsenal

Mark Jenkins

Account Director

Equipping a single professional athlete requires precision. Equipping an entire youth academy of 300 aspiring cricketers in under three weeks requires an industrial miracle. Here is how SportsPro X partnered with the Titans Academy to completely overhaul their sporting infrastructure.

The Titans Academy in Yorkshire had a problem. Their enrollment had doubled, but their equipment stock consisted of mismatched pads, severely degraded nets, and bats that had lost their ping seasons ago. They needed a top-to-bottom refresh before the summer camps commenced.

The Audit and Supply Chain

Our team arrived on-site in late June to conduct a full inventory audit. Within 48 hours, we mapped out a supply chain matrix that included 400 custom sublimated training kits, 50 premium English Willow bats (graded and weighed for different age groups), and high-density foam pads designed for modern fast bowling impacts.

"We thought it was impossible to get custom-branded gear in that timeframe. SportsPro X didn't just deliver boxes; they delivered confidence to 300 young athletes."

Customization at Scale

What sets our bulk fulfillment apart is that we refuse to compromise on customization. Every bat was laser-engraved with the academy's crest, and the moisture-wicking training shirts featured our latest temperature-regulating technology. We didn't just give them stock equipment; we provided them with gear that rivals first-class county teams.

The result? The Titans Academy reported a 30% reduction in equipment-related injuries and a significant boost in overall camp morale. When you look like a professional, you train like one.
